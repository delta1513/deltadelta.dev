Below are my two most favourite pieces from Ron Mueck's Encounter exhibition.

The first Is called Havoc, and it is quite a spectacular display of raw primal nature at work. As soon as I turned the corner in the showroom, I got goosebumps.

The second is called This Little Piggy. This is my true favourite out of all of them because, to me, I feel this conveys a very clear and powerful aphorism. That is, we are all animals, no matter our clothes, possessions, or our means.
